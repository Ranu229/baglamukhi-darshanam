About The Temple

One of the Most Revered Baglamukhi Temples in India

Maa Baglamukhi Temple in Nalkheda is one of the most powerful and spiritually significant temples dedicated to Goddess Baglamukhi — one of the ten Mahavidyas. Thousands of devotees travel from across India to seek the divine blessings of Maa at this sacred shrine. The temple radiates an atmosphere of profound peace, devotion, and divine energy.

🕉️

Sacred Spiritual Environment

A divine atmosphere of deep devotion and spiritual energy surrounds the temple grounds.

Revered for centuries as one of the most potent Shakti peethas in central India.
